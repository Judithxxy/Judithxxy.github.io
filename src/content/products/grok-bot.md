## What it does

- Runs persistent agents on dedicated cloud machines that keep working while your device is off
- Signs into the apps and websites you already use to carry out multi-step tasks
- Works entirely inside a chat thread modeled on iMessage

## Details worth noting

- Onboarding starts from your connected tools, then proposes an agent and a first task
- No "stop" control mid-task — you redirect the agent by sending another message
- Progress status (searching / thinking) stays hidden unless you hover over the working bot
- Labeled early beta at launch

## Who it's for

- People who want to hand real, multi-step work to an agent without building a workflow
- Anyone put off by node-and-settings agent builders
